# 💫 About Me:

I am an enthusiastic and goal-oriented individual with a strong interest in technology, innovation, entrepreneurship, and collaborative problem-solving. My academic background and practical experiences have helped me develop a foundation in web development, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Generative AI, and IoT-based technologies.<br><br>Throughout my academic journey, I have actively participated in technical projects, student communities, and innovation-driven activities. This experience strengthened my leadership, technical coordination, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities.<br><br>I also served as the Club Team & Activities Manager at the InnoveKA Student Community, where I focused on fostering collaboration, encouraging equal participation, facilitating information sharing, and coordinating team activities effectively. These responsibilities helped me understand the importance of communication, organization, accountability, and collective effort in achieving common goals.<br><br>Additionally, I gained professional exposure as a Student Intern at Smartbridge Educational Services, where I worked with software technologies and application concepts to improve project analysis. This experience also helped me strengthen my communication and collaboration skills in a professional working environment.<br><br>My journey in innovation and entrepreneurship has been highlighted by several team achievements. I was part of the **Top 8 winning team at Startup Mahakumbh 2025**, held at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. These experiences have strengthened my interest in transforming ideas into practical solutions through teamwork, technical thinking, and effective presentation.<br><br>Beyond technical knowledge, I value continuous learning, adaptability, independent project execution, and the ability to work effectively with diverse teams. I aspire to combine my technical skills, leadership capabilities, and entrepreneurial mindset to contribute to meaningful projects, develop innovative technology-driven solutions, and create a positive impact through technology.<br><br>My long-term objective is to grow as a technology professional who can bridge the gap between technical innovation and real-world needs while continuously expanding my knowledge, practical experience, and leadership potential.
